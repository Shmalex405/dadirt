# Corners: the line the rider takes builds the track.
#   A. a circle on the flat loam pad, 8 m radius, lap after lap at a gentle throttle:
#      the line packs and sinks and the sheared layer goes to the outside as a
#      shoulder; then harder so the tyre runs at a bigger slip angle.
#   B. the testbed berm arc at (56, 0), 34 deg bank: ten passes through the bank on
#      the same line, entering at the arc start and braking before its far end.
#   C. the real track: the wheel follows the centre line (DaDirt.Lap) through the
#      tightest corner (17 m radius at (162, -43)) and on round the lap.
# DaDirt.Orbit steers the wheel round a circle; the probes read a radial cross-section
# through the line (rut floor, inner shoulder, outer shoulder) as the laps go by.
# Status lines carry lateral N, slip angle, dyn g, impact g and shoved litres.

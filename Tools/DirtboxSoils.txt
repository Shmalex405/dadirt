# The soils. Each reference soil in turn is painted over the pad and asked the
# same four questions: how steep does a loose cone stand, how far does the tyre
# sink and how well does it grip, how fast does a puddle soak in, how much dust
# does a burnout make. Then the quilt: one drive across all five lanes.
# The pad runs Y = -30..30 at X = 3; the quilt is Y = 20..30, lanes across X.
# The repose cone is measured in the whole box (12.5 cm cells): at 3.9 cm cells a
# cone takes far longer to settle than the 3 s the test waits. The cone is read as a
# profile along +Y from its centre; the max-slope statistic is the block's cohesive
# step, not the cone.
wait 2 startup
DaDirt.Mode testbed
DaDirt.Reset
wait 1
DaDirt.Focus 3 -5 40
wait 1
DaDirt.Soil

# ---- one block per soil ---------------------------------------------------------
DaDirt.Focus off
DaDirt.Soil sand
DaDirt.Test repose

# Erosion: run-off carrying dirt. A 1.2 m loam mound on the pad (22 deg flanks)
# is rained on at 60 mm/min for 60 s (erosion runs at the water's game pace, x10). The water runs off its flanks, shears the
# bed, carries grains down and drops them at the toe. Probes read the crest, the
# flank and the toe before and after; the audit counts the dirt in the water.
# Then the same mound in sand (moves easily, settles in a metre) and in clay
# (needs 8 Pa of shear, stays in suspension). Pad X = 3; the mound is at Y = -12.
wait 2 startup
DaDirt.Mode testbed
sun -22 35
DaDirt.Reset
wait 1
DaDirt.Focus 3 -5 40
wait 1

# --- loam --------------------------------------------------------------------------
DaDirt.Raise 3 -12 300 120
wait 4 loam_mound
DaDirt.Probe 18 -20
DaDirt.Probe 3 -12
DaDirt.Probe 3 -10.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 -9.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 -8.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 -7.5
DaDirt.Audit
camera 3 -3 2.2 -16 270
wait 0.4
screenshot erosion_00_loam_mound
DaDirt.Rain 60 60
wait 30 loam_raining
DaDirt.Probe 3 -10.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 -8.5
DaDirt.Probe 18 -20
DaDirt.Audit
screenshot erosion_01_loam_raining
wait 40 loam_rained
DaDirt.Probe 18 -20
DaDirt.Probe 3 -12
DaDirt.Probe 3 -10.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 -9.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 -8.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 -7.5
DaDirt.Audit
screenshot erosion_02_loam_after

# --- sand ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
DaDirt.Soil sand
DaDirt.Reset
wait 1
DaDirt.Raise 3 -12 300 120
wait 4 sand_mound
DaDirt.Probe 3 -12
DaDirt.Probe 3 -9.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 -7.5
DaDirt.Audit
DaDirt.Rain 60 60
wait 30 sand_raining
DaDirt.Audit
wait 40 sand_rained
DaDirt.Probe 3 -12
DaDirt.Probe 3 -9.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 -7.5
DaDirt.Audit
screenshot erosion_03_sand_after

# --- clay ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
DaDirt.Soil clay
DaDirt.Reset
wait 1
DaDirt.Raise 3 -12 300 120
wait 4 clay_mound
DaDirt.Probe 3 -12
DaDirt.Probe 3 -9.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 -7.5
DaDirt.Audit
DaDirt.Rain 60 60
wait 30 clay_raining
DaDirt.Audit
wait 40 clay_rained
DaDirt.Probe 3 -12
DaDirt.Probe 3 -9.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 -7.5
DaDirt.Audit
screenshot erosion_04_clay_after

# --- a rut washing out: loam, a rut over the mound, then rain ---------------------------
DaDirt.Soil built
DaDirt.Reset
wait 1
DaDirt.Raise 3 -12 300 120
wait 4
DaDirt.Wheel 3 -22 90
DaDirt.Drive 0.5 0 0
wait 5 rut_over_mound
DaDirt.Drive 0 0 1
wait 1
DaDirt.Probe 3 -16
DaDirt.Probe 3.2 -16
DaDirt.Probe 3 -14
DaDirt.Probe 3.2 -14
DaDirt.Rain 60 60
wait 70 rut_rained
DaDirt.Probe 3 -16
DaDirt.Probe 3.2 -16
DaDirt.Probe 3 -14
DaDirt.Probe 3.2 -14
DaDirt.Audit
camera 3 -4 1.6 -18 270
wait 0.4
screenshot erosion_05_rut_washout
quit

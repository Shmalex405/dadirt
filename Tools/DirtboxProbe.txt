# DaDirt targeted physics probes. Answers two questions the checklist could not:
#   1. does loose dirt actually settle to the 32 deg angle of repose?
#   2. does saturated dirt (mud) slump flatter than dry dirt?
# Everything is measured with DaDirt.Probe height samples, so the answer is a
# number in the log rather than a picture.
#
# Launch like DirtboxChecklist.txt, with -DirtScript=Tools/DirtboxProbe.txt

wait 2 startup
DaDirt.Mode testbed
DaDirt.View
wait 1

# --- A: the testbed's own loose cone at (3, -45), before and after settling ------
DaDirt.Pause 1
DaDirt.Reset
wait 0.5
# profile along +X from the apex, paused (t = 0)
DaDirt.Probe 3 -45
DaDirt.Probe 3.5 -45
DaDirt.Probe 4 -45
DaDirt.Probe 4.5 -45
DaDirt.Probe 5 -45
DaDirt.Probe 5.5 -45
DaDirt.Probe 6 -45
DaDirt.Probe 7 -45
DaDirt.Probe 8 -45
DaDirt.Probe 10 -45
DaDirt.Pause 0
wait 5 cone_settle
# same profile after 5 s of slumping
DaDirt.Probe 3 -45
DaDirt.Probe 3.5 -45
DaDirt.Probe 4 -45
DaDirt.Probe 4.5 -45
DaDirt.Probe 5 -45
DaDirt.Probe 5.5 -45
DaDirt.Probe 6 -45
DaDirt.Probe 7 -45
DaDirt.Probe 8 -45
DaDirt.Probe 10 -45
DaDirt.Audit

# --- B: a fresh loose pile on the flat pad: 100 cm high over a 150 cm core ---------
# peak slope of that kernel is about 45 deg, so it must slump down to ~32 deg
DaDirt.Pause 1
DaDirt.Reset
wait 0.5
DaDirt.Loosen 3 0 600 1
DaDirt.Raise 3 0 150 100
wait 0.5
DaDirt.Probe 3 0
DaDirt.Probe 3.25 0
DaDirt.Probe 3.5 0
DaDirt.Probe 3.75 0
DaDirt.Probe 4 0
DaDirt.Probe 4.25 0
DaDirt.Probe 4.5 0
DaDirt.Probe 4.75 0
DaDirt.Probe 5 0
DaDirt.Probe 5.5 0
DaDirt.Probe 6 0
DaDirt.Probe 7 0
DaDirt.Pause 0
wait 5 pile_settle
DaDirt.Probe 3 0
DaDirt.Probe 3.25 0
DaDirt.Probe 3.5 0
DaDirt.Probe 3.75 0
DaDirt.Probe 4 0
DaDirt.Probe 4.25 0
DaDirt.Probe 4.5 0
DaDirt.Probe 4.75 0
DaDirt.Probe 5 0
DaDirt.Probe 5.5 0
DaDirt.Probe 6 0
DaDirt.Probe 7 0
DaDirt.Audit

# --- C: mud vs dry: two identical steep piles, one soaked to saturation -----------
DaDirt.Pause 1
DaDirt.Reset
wait 0.5
DaDirt.Raise 3 -15 150 100
DaDirt.Raise 3 15 150 100
DaDirt.Wet 3 15 800 1
DaDirt.Pause 0
wait 6 mud_settle
# dry pile
DaDirt.Probe 3 -15
DaDirt.Probe 3.5 -15
DaDirt.Probe 4 -15
DaDirt.Probe 4.5 -15
DaDirt.Probe 5 -15
DaDirt.Probe 6 -15
DaDirt.Probe 7 -15
# wet pile
DaDirt.Probe 3 15
DaDirt.Probe 3.5 15
DaDirt.Probe 4 15
DaDirt.Probe 4.5 15
DaDirt.Probe 5 15
DaDirt.Probe 6 15
DaDirt.Probe 7 15
DaDirt.DebugView 3
wait 1
screenshot probe_moisture
DaDirt.DebugView 0
DaDirt.Audit

# --- D: track ledger with the corrected borrow pit sizing ---------------------------
DaDirt.Mode track
wait 2
DaDirt.Info
quit

# Off the pad: the wheel against every shape the testbed has. The window
# follows the wheel at rut resolution (3.9 cm cells) the whole way.
#   A. the 7.5 m dome at (22, 30): a 40 deg cosine face, crest, descent, brake
#   B. the SX whoops at Y=+60 (4.3 m / 90 cm) at speed, then the FIM rolling
#      waves at Y=-60 (10 m / 80 cm)
#   C. the jump lineup: the 32 deg face at Y=-16 flat out, the flight, the landing
#   D. the angle spectrum: standing starts up the packed 25 deg and 40 deg wedges
#   E. the berm arc at (56, 0): a left-hander banked 34 deg at 1.1 m
#   F. the loose 3 m mound at (54, 42) and the 3 m bowl at (54, -42)
# Status lines carry speed, slip, sink, heap and air time. Heading 0 = +X, 90 = +Y.

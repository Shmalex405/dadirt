# DaDirt Phase C: water (docs/SoilPhysics.md section 5).
#   1. a water truck pass on the loose pad: it soaks in, the moisture view goes blue,
#      then drains back toward field capacity over a minute
#   2. the same on a packed patch: it cannot soak in, so it ponds and runs downhill
#   3. rain on the whole box: ruts and holes fill first, hills shed it
#   4. Proctor: a tyre packs damp dirt faster than dry or soaked dirt
# The pad runs Y = -30..30 at X = 3, surface 60 cm.

wait 2 startup
DaDirt.Mode testbed
sun -22 35
DaDirt.Reset
wait 1

# --- 1. water truck on loose dirt ---------------------------------------------------------
DaDirt.Water 3 -15 400 250
wait 1 soak_1s
DaDirt.Probe 3 -15
DaDirt.Probe 3 -13
DaDirt.Audit
camera -3 -15 2.5 -25 0
DaDirt.DebugView 3
wait 0.4
screenshot water_10_truck_loose_moisture
DaDirt.DebugView 0
wait 0.4
screenshot water_11_truck_loose
wait 20 drain_20s
DaDirt.Probe 3 -15
DaDirt.Audit

# --- 2. water truck on packed dirt: ponds --------------------------------------------------
DaDirt.Pack 3 15 400 1
wait 0.5
DaDirt.Dig 3 15 150 8
wait 1
DaDirt.Water 3 15 400 250
wait 1 pond_1s
DaDirt.Probe 3 15
DaDirt.Probe 3 17
camera -3 15 2.5 -25 0
DaDirt.DebugView 3
wait 0.4
screenshot water_20_truck_packed_moisture
DaDirt.DebugView 0
wait 0.4
screenshot water_21_truck_packed_pond
wait 10 pond_10s
DaDirt.Probe 3 15
DaDirt.Audit

# --- 3. rain -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
DaDirt.Reset
wait 0.5
DaDirt.Test trench
wait 2
DaDirt.Rain 60 8
wait 10 rain
DaDirt.Probe 3 0
DaDirt.Probe 5 0
DaDirt.Probe 3 -45
camera -10 0 6 -30 0
DaDirt.DebugView 3
wait 0.4
screenshot water_30_rain_moisture
DaDirt.DebugView 0
wait 0.4
screenshot water_31_rain
DaDirt.Audit
wait 20 after_rain
DaDirt.Probe 3 0
DaDirt.Audit

# --- 4. Proctor: pack three patches with a tyre-style (Proctor) pack -------------------------
# The console Pack ignores the curve, so use the wheel: three short runs on dry,
# damp and soaked ground. Compaction after one pass should be highest on damp.
DaDirt.Reset
wait 0.5
DaDirt.Wet 3 -20 300 0.6
DaDirt.Wet 3 20 300 1.5
wait 1
DaDirt.Wheel 3 -28 90
DaDirt.Drive 0.35 0 0
wait 9 proctor_run
DaDirt.Drive 0 0 1
wait 1
DaDirt.Probe 3 -20
DaDirt.Probe 3 0
DaDirt.Probe 3 20
DaDirt.Audit
quit

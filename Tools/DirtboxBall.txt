# DaDirt ball test: gravity meets dirt.
#   1. a 30 cm ball dropped 5 m onto the flat pad     -> dent, bounce, rest in the dent
#   2. a 60 cm ball dropped 12 m onto the pad          -> bigger dent
#   3. a 30 cm ball dropped on the flank of the 11 m dome -> rolls downhill, grooves, stops
#   4. a 30 cm ball thrown across the pad at 6 m/s     -> skips and rolls to a stop
# The rest positions are logged by the balls; probes confirm the dents.

wait 2 startup
DaDirt.Mode testbed
sun -22 35
DaDirt.Reset
wait 1

DaDirt.Ball 3 -10 5 30
DaDirt.Ball 3 10 12 60
DaDirt.Ball 36 -32 2 30
DaDirt.Ball -3 24 1 30 6 0
camera -9 0 4 -18 0
wait 0.4
screenshot ball_00_falling
wait 0.6
screenshot ball_01_landing
wait 6 balls_settle
screenshot ball_02_rest
camera -1 -10 1.6 -22 0
wait 0.4
screenshot ball_10_small_closeup
camera 0 10 2.4 -24 0
wait 0.4
screenshot ball_11_big_closeup
camera 30 -32 6 -20 0
wait 0.4
screenshot ball_12_dome_closeup
camera -8 24 2 -14 0
wait 0.4
screenshot ball_13_thrown_closeup

# dents: pad surface is 60 cm; under each resting ball it should be lower
DaDirt.Probe 3 -10
DaDirt.Probe 3.6 -10
DaDirt.Probe 3 10
DaDirt.Probe 4.2 10
DaDirt.Audit
DaDirt.DebugView 1
wait 0.4
camera -9 0 4 -18 0
wait 0.4
screenshot ball_20_layer_depth
DaDirt.DebugView 0
quit

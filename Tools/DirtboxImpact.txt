# Impact compaction: a landing is Proctor's hammer.
#   A. drop tests at rut resolution: the wheel dropped 1 m onto the same spot five
#      times, on the loam pad and then on the sand and clay lanes of the quilt.
#      The probes read the crater depth and the compaction under it after each hit.
#   B. the 32 deg jump flat out, three times over the same landing: the landing
#      ramp packs and hollows where the tyre comes down.
#   C. the 3 m bowl at 10 m/s: g-out at the bottom packs it (the rolling dynamic load).
# Status lines carry "impact <g> <cm>" for the last landing and "dyn <g>" while rolling.
# The pad's loam is at X = 3; the quilt lanes at Y = 20..30 are sand (X -4.2),
# granite (-0.6), loam (3), pnw (6.6), clay (10.2).
wait 2 startup

DaDirt.Reset
wait 1

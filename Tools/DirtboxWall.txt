# DaDirt: do parcels that hit the edge of a focused region keep their books?
# A 40 m region centred on (3, 0) has its -Y wall at Y = -20. Throw the same
# 10 L at the wall, and away from it, and audit after everything has landed.

wait 2 startup
DaDirt.Mode testbed
DaDirt.Reset
wait 1
DaDirt.Focus 3 0 40
DaDirt.Shed 0
wait 2
DaDirt.Audit

# --- K. into the wall: from Y = -15 toward -Y at 8 m/s, lands at the wall --------------
DaDirt.Throw 3 -15 10 8 -90 30 8
wait 0.5
DaDirt.Parcels
wait 5 K_wall
DaDirt.Parcels
DaDirt.Audit

# --- L. same throw into open ground -------------------------------------------------------
DaDirt.Reset
wait 1
DaDirt.Throw 3 -5 10 8 90 30 8
wait 5.5 L_open
DaDirt.Parcels
DaDirt.Audit

# --- M. the wall again, twice as hard -------------------------------------------------------
DaDirt.Reset
wait 1
DaDirt.Throw 3 -15 20 12 -90 20 8
wait 5.5 M_wall_hard
DaDirt.Parcels
DaDirt.Audit
quit
